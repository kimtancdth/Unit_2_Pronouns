📚 Grammar Reminder – Let's Learn!

Subject pronouns

I – tôi · You – bạn
He – anh ấy · She – cô ấy
It – nó · We – chúng tôi
They – họ / chúng

Change I / We → They

I am a student. → They are students.

We play football. → They play football.

Remember:

I am / We are → They are · My / Our → Their

I / We have → They have · I / We don't → They don't

When changing one person to several people, change a singular noun to plural when needed.
